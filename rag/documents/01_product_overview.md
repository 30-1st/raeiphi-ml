# RAEIPHI — Product Overview

## What is RAEIPHI?

RAEIPHI is a WiFi-based occupancy sensing platform built for the hospitality and property management industry. It detects how many people are in a property or space in real time — without cameras, microphones, wearables, or any recording devices. No one needs to carry anything or install an app. The system works passively using the physics of WiFi signals.

RAEIPHI uses a technology called WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) sensing. Every WiFi signal traveling between devices carries detailed data about the environment it passes through. When people are present in a room, their bodies reflect, absorb, and scatter these signals in measurable ways. RAEIPHI's hardware nodes capture these signal disturbances and a trained machine learning model interprets them to determine how many people are present.

The result is a real-time occupancy count for every room in a property, accessible through a cloud-based SaaS dashboard with alerts, historical data, and an AI assistant.

## Core Value Proposition

Property operators across hospitality, short-term rentals, and commercial real estate share a common problem: they have no reliable, privacy-respecting way to know how many people are actually in their spaces at any given time. Guest limits go unenforced. Spaces are over- or under-utilized. Operational decisions are made on booking data that doesn't reflect reality.

Existing solutions are inadequate. Cameras raise privacy concerns, are banned inside rental properties on most platforms, and create legal liability in hospitality settings. Motion sensors only detect presence, not headcount. Noise monitors catch disturbances after they escalate. Badge systems count swipes, not actual occupancy. None of these tell you how many people are actually there right now.

RAEIPHI fills this gap across three primary markets: staffless hospitality, short-term rental hosting, and flexible workspace management. It counts occupants accurately, privately, and continuously. No images. No audio. No personally identifiable information collected. Just a number: how many people are in the space right now.

## How It Works (Simple Version)

1. You install RAEIPHI hardware nodes in your property — small devices in custom casings that plug into standard power outlets.
2. The nodes communicate with each other over WiFi, continuously measuring the signal characteristics between them.
3. When people enter, move through, or occupy the space, the WiFi signals between nodes change in predictable, measurable ways.
4. A machine learning model trained on these signal patterns determines the occupancy count in real time.
5. The count is sent to the RAEIPHI cloud platform, where you can view it on your dashboard, receive alerts, and review historical occupancy data.

## Privacy First

RAEIPHI is designed around privacy from the ground up. The system does not capture images, video, audio, or any biometric data. It does not identify who is in the room — only how many people are present. There is nothing to record, nothing to store, and nothing that could be used to identify an individual.

This matters across every market RAEIPHI serves. Short-term rental platforms like Airbnb explicitly prohibit surveillance devices inside rental properties. Hotels face legal liability for monitoring guest rooms. Co-working tenants reject cameras in shared workspaces. Staffless hospitality operations need monitoring without the privacy backlash that surveillance creates. RAEIPHI complies fully in every scenario because it is not a surveillance device — it is a physics-based sensing system that captures radio signal measurements, not sensory recordings.

## Primary Markets

### Staffless Hotels and Staffless Hospitality

The staffless hospitality market is growing rapidly. Operators run fully automated hotels, apart-hotels, and serviced apartments with no front desk, no concierge, and no on-site staff. Guests check in via smart locks and apps, and the entire operation runs remotely.

The problem with staffless operations is the complete absence of human eyes on the property. Traditional hotels have front desk staff who notice when a room booked for two has eight people arriving. A housekeeping team walking the halls can spot unusual activity. In a staffless property, none of this exists.

RAEIPHI becomes the operator's eyes without being surveillance. It provides the occupancy awareness that staffless operations lose when they remove on-site personnel. Specific capabilities for staffless hospitality include:

**Guest limit enforcement without confrontation.** The system detects unauthorized occupants automatically. The operator can contact the guest remotely, adjust billing, or escalate — all before damage occurs. No staff needed on-site.

**Automated check-in and check-out verification.** Smart locks tell you when a door opens, not whether the guest actually arrived and is staying. RAEIPHI confirms actual human presence, enabling accurate turnover scheduling and cleaning dispatch.

**Remote operational intelligence.** Staffless operators manage properties from anywhere — often across multiple cities or countries. The RAEIPHI dashboard provides the same occupancy awareness they would have with on-site staff, centralized in one interface.

**Safety compliance at scale.** Fire codes, building regulations, and insurance policies all specify occupancy limits. Without staff to enforce them, the operator needs an automated system. RAEIPHI provides documented, real-time compliance monitoring.

**Incident prevention.** Unauthorized parties, excessive occupancy, and property abuse are the primary risks in staffless hospitality. RAEIPHI detects the conditions that precede these incidents — rising headcount, unexpected overnight occupants, occupancy spikes — and alerts the operator before damage occurs.

The collaboration model between RAEIPHI and staffless hospitality operators is straightforward: RAEIPHI provides the monitoring layer that replaces the awareness lost by removing staff. The operator maintains full remote control, and RAEIPHI provides the data foundation for automated decision-making — triggering alerts, adjusting pricing, dispatching cleaning, and documenting compliance.

### Short-Term Rental Hosts (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com)

Short-term rental hosts set guest limits on their listings but have no way to enforce them. A listing booked for 2 guests may have 8 people show up. Unauthorized guests cause excess wear on the property, noise complaints from neighbors, HOA violations, increased utility costs, and potential liability. The most serious scenario — an unauthorized party — can result in thousands of dollars in property damage.

RAEIPHI gives hosts real-time visibility into occupancy without violating platform rules or guest privacy. If a listing is booked for 2 guests and the system detects 7, the host knows immediately and can take action before the situation escalates.

Specific scenarios RAEIPHI addresses for hosts:
- Party prevention through early detection of occupancy spikes
- Guest count verification against booking data
- Check-in and check-out confirmation for turnover coordination
- Vacancy monitoring between bookings to detect unauthorized access
- Insurance documentation through historical occupancy records

### Property Managers

Property managers overseeing multiple rental units face the same problems as individual hosts, multiplied across their entire portfolio. They cannot physically be at every property. RAEIPHI's multi-property dashboard gives them a single view of occupancy across all units, with per-property alert configuration, centralized historical data, and scalable monitoring that adds new properties in minutes.

### Co-Working and Flexible Office Spaces

Co-working operators sell desk access, private offices, and meeting rooms — but have no reliable way to measure actual utilization. A meeting room booked for 8 might have 2 people in it. A hot desk zone might show "full" in the booking system while sitting at 20% actual occupancy. Real estate decisions — how much space to lease, how to price memberships, how to lay out floors — are made on booking data that doesn't reflect reality.

Cameras in workspaces face strong pushback from tenants. Badge swipes count entries but not how many people are actually at desks or in meeting rooms. WiFi sensing provides real utilization data without watching anyone.

RAEIPHI serves co-working operators by providing:

**Real-time space utilization.** Know how many people are actually at desks, in meeting rooms, and in common areas at any given moment. Compare actual utilization against booked capacity to identify waste and opportunity.

**Meeting room optimization.** Detect rooms that are booked but empty, rooms that are over-capacity, and rooms that are consistently underutilized. Implement automatic release of booked-but-empty rooms after a grace period.

**Dynamic pricing support.** Actual utilization data enables demand-based pricing for hot desks and meeting rooms. Price premium during genuine peak hours, offer discounts during measured low-utilization periods.

**Lease and expansion decisions.** Real occupancy data across an entire location provides the evidence base for whether to expand, consolidate, or renegotiate a lease. Decisions based on actual headcount data, not booking system proxies.

**Tenant reporting.** For enterprise tenants leasing dedicated space within a co-working facility, utilization data provides transparency on how their space is being used, supporting contract renewals and space right-sizing.

### Hotels (Traditional and Boutique)

Hotel operators can use RAEIPHI for room occupancy verification, cleaning scheduling based on actual vacancy, conference and event space headcount monitoring, and energy management. Knowing which rooms are occupied and how many guests are present enables operational efficiency.

### Homeowners

Homeowners interested in home security and presence detection can use RAEIPHI as a non-invasive way to know whether someone is in their home. This is useful for security monitoring, elderly care check-ins, and energy automation.

## What Makes RAEIPHI Different

Unlike camera-based systems, RAEIPHI captures no visual data. Unlike noise monitors, RAEIPHI detects occupancy before a disturbance occurs. Unlike motion sensors, RAEIPHI provides an actual headcount, not just a binary occupied/empty reading. Unlike wearable or app-based tracking, RAEIPHI requires nothing from the occupant.

The system is also extensible. The same WiFi sensing infrastructure can be extended to detect water leaks, fire conditions, and noise levels — all from the same hardware nodes, requiring no additional installation. A property owner who deploys RAEIPHI for occupancy monitoring today gains additional sensing capabilities through software updates as they are released.

## Senior Living and Memory Care (Future Market)

WiFi-based presence sensing has a natural application in senior living facilities and memory care environments. Non-invasive presence monitoring — knowing whether a resident is in their room, detecting potential falls through sudden signal disruption, and tracking activity patterns over time — provides peace of mind for families without the privacy invasion of cameras in a person's living space.

This market has a longer sales cycle and different regulatory requirements than hospitality, so it is not a primary market for RAEIPHI today. However, the same technology and hardware infrastructure applies directly, and RAEIPHI intends to serve this market as the platform matures.
