# RAEIPHI — Pricing and Packages

## Overview

RAEIPHI is sold as a DIY hardware kit paired with a monthly SaaS subscription. There are two hardware purchase options and one subscription tier. All pricing is transparent and scales proportionally with the number of nodes a property requires.

## Hardware Options

### DIY Node — $49 per node (+ shipping and handling)

A single RAEIPHI sensing node. This is the individual hardware unit — an ESP32 and Raspberry Pi in a custom RAEIPHI-branded casing. Intended for customers who are comfortable with self-installation and want to purchase exactly the number of nodes they need. Each node plugs into a standard power outlet and connects to the property's existing WiFi network.

The DIY node option is best for technically comfortable hosts who already know how many nodes they need, want to expand an existing installation, or prefer to buy incrementally.

### Full Package — $89 per package (+ shipping and handling)

A complete setup kit that includes a RAEIPHI sensing node, printed installation instructions, placement guidelines for optimal sensing coverage, and a login to the RAEIPHI SaaS platform. This is the recommended option for first-time customers who want a guided setup experience.

The full package is best for customers setting up RAEIPHI for the first time, those who want clear guidance on where to place nodes, and anyone who values a complete out-of-box experience.

## SaaS Subscription — $19.99 per month

The RAEIPHI cloud platform subscription. This provides access to the real-time occupancy dashboard, alert configuration, historical occupancy data, multi-property management, and the RAEIPHI AI assistant. The subscription covers all nodes and all properties under a single account — there is no per-node or per-property subscription fee.

The subscription includes all software updates, model improvements, and new features as they are released. There are no tiers within the subscription — every subscriber gets the full platform.

## How Many Nodes Do I Need?

For minimum viable accuracy in occupancy detection, each property should have at least 4 nodes. This provides sufficient signal coverage and cross-node measurement diversity for the machine learning model to produce reliable occupancy counts.

The number of nodes scales with property size and layout complexity:

**Studio or small one-bedroom apartment:** 4 nodes is the minimum and is typically sufficient. Place nodes in the main living area and bedroom to ensure full coverage.

**Two-bedroom apartment or small house:** 4 to 5 nodes. The additional node covers the second bedroom or a large common area.

**Three-bedroom house:** 5 to 6 nodes. Larger floor plans and multiple separated rooms require additional coverage points.

**Large house or multi-level property:** 6 to 8 nodes. Each level should have at least 2 nodes, with additional nodes for large open areas or rooms separated by thick walls.

**Multi-unit buildings or commercial properties:** Varies by unit count and layout. Contact RAEIPHI for a custom configuration recommendation and volume pricing.

## Custom and Volume Pricing

For deployments larger than a single property — property management companies, hotel operators, multi-unit buildings, or any scenario requiring more than 8 nodes — RAEIPHI offers custom pricing.

Custom pricing takes into account the number of properties, total node count, deployment timeline, and any specialized installation or integration requirements. The RAEIPHI AI assistant can discuss custom pricing options and help determine the right configuration for your specific needs.

Volume pricing is available for large orders. The per-node cost decreases with quantity, and multi-year subscription commitments may qualify for discounted monthly rates.
