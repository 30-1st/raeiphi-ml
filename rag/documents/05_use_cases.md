# RAEIPHI — Use Cases

## Staffless Hotels and Staffless Hospitality

### The Problem
Staffless hospitality is one of the fastest growing segments in the accommodation industry. Operators run fully automated hotels, apart-hotels, capsule hotels, and serviced apartments with no front desk staff, no concierge, and no on-site personnel. The entire guest experience — booking, check-in, room access, check-out — is handled through apps, smart locks, and automation.

This model dramatically reduces operating costs, but it creates a critical blind spot: no human being is watching the property. In a traditional hotel, front desk staff notice when four people check in under a booking for one. Housekeeping walking the floors spots unusual activity. Security can respond to noise complaints in minutes. In a staffless operation, none of this exists.

The operator is managing the property entirely from a dashboard — and that dashboard currently shows bookings, door lock activity, and maybe a noise reading. It does not show how many people are actually inside.

### How RAEIPHI Solves It
RAEIPHI replaces the human awareness that staffless operations sacrifice for efficiency. It provides the occupancy intelligence layer that sits between the booking system and the physical reality of the property.

**Real-time occupancy across all rooms.** The operator sees a live headcount for every unit in their staffless property from a single dashboard. A unit booked for 2 showing 8 occupants at midnight triggers an immediate alert. No staff required to observe this — the system detects it automatically.

**Automated compliance monitoring.** Fire codes, building regulations, short-term rental permits, and insurance policies specify occupancy limits. In a staffless property, there is no one to enforce them. RAEIPHI provides continuous, documented monitoring that the operator can reference for regulatory compliance, insurance claims, and dispute resolution.

**Cleaning and turnover automation.** Smart locks tell the operator when a door was opened. RAEIPHI tells them when the unit is actually empty. This distinction matters — a guest may open the door to leave for an errand and return, or they may have checked out. Occupancy data enables accurate cleaning dispatch, reducing wasted trips and enabling tighter booking windows.

**Damage prevention at scale.** The primary financial risk in staffless hospitality is property damage from unauthorized parties and excessive occupancy. RAEIPHI detects the conditions that precede damage — rising headcount, unexpected late-night occupancy, occupancy far exceeding the booking — and alerts the operator while there is still time to intervene remotely.

**Guest experience without surveillance.** Staffless hospitality guests choose these properties specifically for independence and privacy. Cameras would undermine the entire value proposition. RAEIPHI monitors occupancy without surveillance, preserving the guest experience while giving the operator the information they need.

### The Collaboration Model
RAEIPHI integrates into the staffless hospitality tech stack alongside the property management system, smart locks, and booking channels. The operator deploys nodes during initial property setup — same installation as any smart device, no technical staff needed. From that point, RAEIPHI operates autonomously, providing occupancy data to the operator's existing dashboard or through its own platform.

For staffless hospitality operators managing multiple properties across multiple cities, RAEIPHI scales with the portfolio. Adding a new property means installing nodes and connecting to the platform. The $19.99/month subscription covers every property and every node under a single account.

## Short-Term Rental Hosts (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com)

### The Problem
Short-term rental hosts set guest limits on their listings, but have no way to enforce them. A listing booked for 2 guests may have 8 people show up. Unauthorized guests cause excess wear on the property, noise complaints from neighbors, HOA violations, increased utility costs, and potential liability for the host. The most serious scenario — an unauthorized party — can result in thousands of dollars in property damage.

Airbnb's own policies prohibit indoor surveillance cameras, so hosts cannot install cameras to monitor guest counts. Outdoor cameras only show who enters the front door and cannot count occupants reliably.

### How RAEIPHI Helps
RAEIPHI provides a real-time occupancy count that the host can monitor remotely. If a listing is booked for 2 guests and the system detects 7, the host knows immediately and can take action before the situation escalates — contact the guest, alert a co-host or property manager, or notify the platform.

Specific scenarios RAEIPHI addresses for hosts:
- **Party prevention:** Detect sudden occupancy spikes (2 guests to 15 in an hour) and receive an immediate alert, often before noise complaints start.
- **Guest count verification:** Confirm that the number of guests matches the booking. A listing booked for 4 with 4 detected occupants is operating as expected.
- **Check-in confirmation:** Know when guests actually arrive without asking for a check-in message. Coordinate cleaning and turnover timing based on actual departure.
- **Vacancy monitoring:** Between bookings, any detected presence triggers an alert. Know if someone enters your property when it should be empty.
- **Insurance documentation:** Historical occupancy data provides evidence of compliance with guest limits, which can support insurance claims or dispute resolution with platforms.

## Property Managers

### The Problem
Property managers overseeing multiple short-term rental units face the host problems multiplied across their entire portfolio. They cannot physically be at every property. They rely on guests to self-report issues, neighbors to call about noise, and cleaning crews to report damage after the fact. By the time they learn about an unauthorized party, the damage is done.

### How RAEIPHI Helps
The multi-property dashboard gives property managers a single view of occupancy across all their units. At a glance, they can see which properties are occupied, how many people are in each, and whether any properties are over their threshold.

For property managers, RAEIPHI's value goes beyond party prevention:
- **Turnover efficiency:** Know exactly when a property is vacated so cleaning crews can be dispatched immediately, reducing turnover time and enabling tighter booking windows.
- **Operational planning:** Historical occupancy data reveals patterns — which properties are consistently over-occupied, which have frequent early check-outs, which sit empty longer than expected.
- **Owner reporting:** For managers operating properties on behalf of owners, occupancy data provides transparency. Owners can see that their property is being used as booked and managed responsibly.
- **Scalable monitoring:** Adding a new property to the RAEIPHI platform takes minutes. No new subscription tier, no per-property fee — the same account covers the entire portfolio.

## Co-Working and Flexible Office Spaces

### The Problem
Co-working operators sell desk access, private offices, and meeting rooms based on booking and membership systems. But booking data does not reflect reality. Meeting rooms are booked and left empty. Hot desk zones show "full" while chairs sit vacant. Enterprise tenants lease private suites based on headcount projections that may be wildly off. Without real utilization data, operators make expensive real estate decisions — expanding, renovating, renegotiating leases — on guesswork.

Cameras are not viable in workspaces. Employees and tenants push back against surveillance in their working environment. Badge swipes count entries at the door but cannot tell you how many people are at desks right now.

### How RAEIPHI Helps
RAEIPHI provides actual occupancy data across the entire co-working facility without surveillance:

- **Real-time utilization dashboard:** See how many people are in each zone — hot desks, private offices, meeting rooms, common areas — at any moment. Compare actual versus booked occupancy in real time.
- **Meeting room optimization:** Automatically detect rooms that are booked but empty. After a configurable grace period, release the room back to the available pool. Identify which rooms are consistently overbooked versus underused.
- **Space planning with real data:** Make expansion, contraction, and layout decisions based on measured occupancy patterns, not booking proxies. Know peak hours, peak zones, and actual density across the floor plan.
- **Dynamic pricing:** Use real utilization data to implement demand-based pricing. Premium pricing during measured peak hours. Discounts during genuinely underutilized periods. Hot desk pricing that reflects actual demand rather than arbitrary tiers.
- **Enterprise tenant reporting:** Provide tenants with utilization reports showing how their dedicated space is being used. Support contract renewals, right-sizing conversations, and upsell opportunities with data.
- **Energy and HVAC integration:** Occupancy data drives building systems — reduce HVAC and lighting in zones with no detected occupancy, increase in high-traffic areas. Reduce operating costs without sacrificing comfort.

## Hotel Operators

### The Problem
Hotels struggle with room occupancy accuracy beyond the check-in desk. Double occupancy rooms may host four people. Dayrooms booked for meetings may overflow capacity. Conference spaces are booked based on estimated attendance, not actual headcount. Housekeeping schedules are based on checkout time, not actual occupancy, leading to wasted labor or delayed cleaning.

### How RAEIPHI Helps
RAEIPHI can be deployed in hotel rooms, conference spaces, and common areas to provide actual occupancy data:
- **Revenue protection:** Detect when rooms booked for 2 are occupied by 4+, enabling accurate surcharge enforcement.
- **Housekeeping optimization:** Deploy cleaning crews based on actual room vacancy rather than scheduled checkout times. Rooms vacated early get cleaned early, improving turnaround.
- **Conference and event management:** Real-time headcount in meeting rooms and event spaces for capacity compliance and safety.
- **Energy management:** Integrate occupancy data with HVAC and lighting systems to reduce energy consumption in unoccupied rooms.

## Homeowners

### The Problem
Homeowners who travel frequently, own vacation homes, or care for elderly family members want to know whether someone is present in their home — but do not want to install cameras throughout their living space.

### How RAEIPHI Helps
RAEIPHI provides a non-invasive presence detection system for residential use:
- **Security monitoring:** Know when someone enters your home while you are away. Unlike motion sensors that trigger false alarms, RAEIPHI provides an occupancy count — a reading jumping from 0 to 1 is a genuine presence change.
- **Elderly care:** Family members can monitor whether their parent is home and active without cameras. A home that shows 0 occupancy during expected waking hours may indicate a fall or departure.
- **Vacation home monitoring:** Detect unauthorized access to seasonal properties outside expected periods.
- **Smart home integration:** Occupancy data can drive HVAC, lighting, and security automations.

## Insurance and Liability

Short-term rental and hospitality insurance providers increasingly require operators to demonstrate due diligence in monitoring their properties. RAEIPHI's historical occupancy data provides documented evidence that occupancy limits were monitored and enforced. In the event of a damage claim or liability dispute, this data can support the operator's position that they took reasonable steps to manage their property.

Some insurance providers may offer reduced premiums for properties with active occupancy monitoring systems. The combination of occupancy monitoring with RAEIPHI's upcoming leak and fire detection capabilities creates a comprehensive risk mitigation package that insurers value.
