# RAEIPHI — Future Capabilities and Roadmap

## The Platform Vision

RAEIPHI's occupancy sensing is the first application built on a broader WiFi sensing platform. The same hardware nodes and WiFi CSI technology that detects human presence can be extended to detect other environmental events — all without installing additional sensors or hardware. Every capability described below uses the nodes you have already installed.

This means a property owner who purchases RAEIPHI for occupancy monitoring today will gain additional sensing capabilities through software updates as they are released. No new hardware purchase required.

## Water Leak and Flood Detection

WiFi signals are highly sensitive to the presence of water. Water has a high dielectric constant, which means it strongly absorbs and reflects radio waves. A pool of water on a floor, a leaking pipe behind a wall, or rising water from a flood all cause measurable changes in the CSI signal patterns.

RAEIPHI's leak detection will work by establishing baseline signal characteristics for a dry property and then detecting deviations consistent with water presence. Because WiFi signals pass through walls, this can detect leaks inside walls, under floors, and in areas that are not directly visible — catching problems before they cause major damage.

For short-term rental hosts, water damage is one of the most expensive risks. A slow leak during a guest stay can go unnoticed for days. RAEIPHI's leak detection will provide early warning, enabling the host or property manager to respond before the damage spreads.

## Fire Detection

Fire and smoke alter the environment in ways that WiFi signals can detect. Rapid temperature changes affect air density and signal propagation. Smoke particles scatter radio waves. The physical disruption of a fire — heat convection, material decomposition, air movement — creates CSI patterns that differ significantly from normal environmental variation.

RAEIPHI's fire detection will complement, not replace, traditional smoke detectors. It adds a redundant detection layer that works on different physical principles. A smoke detector requires smoke to physically reach it. WiFi-based fire sensing can detect environmental disruption across the entire sensing coverage area simultaneously.

This capability is particularly valuable for properties where smoke detectors may have dead batteries, be incorrectly placed, or be tampered with by guests (a known problem in short-term rentals).

## Noise Level Estimation

WiFi CSI can detect vibrations caused by sound, particularly loud music and bass frequencies. These vibrations cause micro-movements in objects and surfaces throughout a room, which create measurable fluctuations in the WiFi signal.

RAEIPHI's noise estimation will provide an approximate sound level reading derived from signal analysis. It will not record audio or capture speech — it detects the physical vibration pattern caused by loud sound, not the sound content itself.

For hosts, noise estimation combined with occupancy counting creates a comprehensive party detection system: 12 people detected plus elevated noise patterns at midnight triggers a high-confidence party alert with more actionable information than either signal alone.

## Environmental Monitoring

Beyond specific event detection, WiFi CSI can provide general environmental monitoring capabilities:

**Room utilization analytics:** For hotel operators and commercial properties, tracking not just whether a room is occupied but how frequently and for how long. This data informs real estate utilization decisions, workspace design, and facility planning.

**Activity level sensing:** Distinguishing between an occupied room where people are stationary (sleeping, sitting) versus active (walking, exercising, cleaning). This can inform energy management and HVAC optimization.

**Occupancy flow patterns:** Understanding how people move through a multi-room property over time. Which rooms are most used, what are the traffic patterns between spaces, and when do usage peaks occur.

## Integration Capabilities

As the RAEIPHI platform matures, integration with other property management and smart home systems will expand:

**Smart home platforms:** Occupancy data driving HVAC, lighting, and security automations through standard smart home protocols.

**Property management software:** Occupancy data feeding into operational dashboards alongside booking data, maintenance schedules, and financial reporting.

**Booking platforms:** Automated occupancy limit enforcement linked to platform booking data, enabling proactive compliance rather than reactive monitoring.

**Insurance platforms:** Real-time occupancy and environmental data shared with insurance providers to support dynamic pricing, risk assessment, and claims processing.

## Senior Living and Memory Care

The same WiFi sensing technology that detects occupancy in hospitality settings has a natural application in senior living facilities and memory care environments. Non-invasive presence monitoring for elderly residents — knowing whether someone is in their room, detecting potential falls through sudden signal disruption patterns, and tracking daily activity patterns over time — provides peace of mind for families without the privacy invasion of cameras in a person's living space.

This market has a longer sales cycle and different regulatory requirements than hospitality and commercial real estate, so it is not a primary market for RAEIPHI today. However, the technology and hardware infrastructure applies directly, and RAEIPHI intends to serve this market as the platform matures.

## Timeline

RAEIPHI's current release focuses exclusively on occupancy detection — the core use case that provides immediate value across staffless hospitality, short-term rentals, and co-working spaces. Future capabilities are in active development. Leak detection is the next priority, followed by noise estimation and fire detection. All future capabilities will be delivered as software updates to existing hardware — no additional nodes or equipment will be required.
